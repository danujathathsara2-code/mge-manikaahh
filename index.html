A NOTE FROM MY HEART

Mage sudu manika, ♡

Randu weddi “api nawaththamu” kiyala oyage hitha ridduwata mata samawenna. Mama awulen hitiyath, e wachana kiyapu eka hari na. Mage wachana walata wagakeema mama gannawa.

Oya mata godak watinawa. Mama oyata adarei. Mata one oyage hitha ridena dewal therum aragena, mage hithath sanasillen oyata kiyanna.

Tharaha giyama ridena wachana kiyanawa wenuwata, poddak nawathila sanasila katha karanna mama mahansi wenawa. Adare wachana walin witharak nemei, mage hasireemenuth pennanna one.

Me page eka haduwe samawa laba ganna balakaranna nemei. Mata waraduna de piliganna, saha oya mata kochchara watinawada kiyanna.

Oya sudanam dawasaka api nidahase katha karamu. Oyata ona kalaya ganna; ikmanin reply ekak denna one na. Mama oyata ahagena innam, mage manikeeh. ♡

Adaren,
Oyagema Danuu
